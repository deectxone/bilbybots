# Years 1–5 — Science topics

Source of truth: Australian Curriculum v9.0, Science F–10 (strands: Science
understanding U, Science as a human endeavour H, Science inquiry I). Every
content description for Years 1–5 is covered below and mirrored in
`apps/mobile/src/data/year{1,2,3,4,5}/science.ts`. Codes verified against
ACARA v9.0 F–6 curriculum content.

Per AC v9.0 the Years 1–5 band carries the following content descriptions
(Science understanding is per-year; H and I are per 2-year band: Y1–2 share
H01 + I01–I06, Y3–4 share H01–H02 + I01–I06, Y5 has H01–H02 + I01–I06 with
Year-5 wording):

| Year | Understanding | Human Endeavour | Inquiry | Total |
|------|--------------:|----------------:|--------:|------:|
| 1    | 3             | 1               | 6       | 10    |
| 2    | 3             | 1               | 6       | 10    |
| 3    | 4             | 2               | 6       | 12    |
| 4    | 4             | 2               | 6       | 12    |
| 5    | 4             | 2               | 6       | 12    |

> **Sensitivity:** ACARA F–6 Science includes First Nations Australian
> elaborations (e.g. caring for living things, seasonal knowledge, traditional
> instruments). Topics touching these are authored with respect and factual
> care; any that involve researching Country/Place carry the `sensitivity`
> flag pending a community-protocol review (see AGENTS.md).

Each topic below is one unit (**lesson + quick-check + assignment**) tagged with
its CD codes. Topic ids follow `Y<year>-SCI-<strand><NN>` (U / H / I). Every
topic ships 6 assignment questions at nominal pace and 4 under compact pace
(adaptive pacing, see `docs/specs/adaptive-pacing.md`). Illustration slots
render real vector diagrams via `src/components/illustrations/diagrams.tsx`.

## Year 1 — Science topics

### Y1-SCI-U01 · Living things & their needs
- CDs: `AC9S1U01`
- Learn: basic needs of plants and animals (air, water, food, shelter); how
  the places they live meet those needs.
- Assignment: 6 questions (needs, habitats, matching places).

### Y1-SCI-U02 · Daily & seasonal changes
- CDs: `AC9S1U02`
- Learn: daily and seasonal changes in the environment; how changes affect
  everyday life (weather, seasons, day/night).
- Assignment: 6 questions (weather, seasons, daily cycles).

### Y1-SCI-U03 · Pushes and pulls
- CDs: `AC9S1U03`
- Learn: pushes and pulls in terms of strength and direction; predict effects
  on motion and shape.
- Assignment: 6 questions (push/pull, strength, direction).

### Y1-SCI-H01 · Science in daily life
- CDs: `AC9S1H01`
- Learn: how people use science in their daily lives, including using patterns
  to make predictions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (science in daily life, patterns→predictions).

### Y1-SCI-I01 · Questions & predictions
- CDs: `AC9S1I01`
- Learn: pose questions to explore simple patterns/relationships; make
  predictions based on experiences.
- Assignment: 6 questions (good questions, predictions).

### Y1-SCI-I02 · Safe investigation procedures
- CDs: `AC9S1I02`
- Learn: suggest and follow safe procedures to investigate questions and test
  predictions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (safety steps, fair-ish testing at this level).

### Y1-SCI-I03 · Observations & recording
- CDs: `AC9S1I03`
- Learn: make and record observations, including informal measurements, using
  digital tools as appropriate.
- Assignment: 6 questions (observe, record, measure informally).

### Y1-SCI-I04 · Sorting data & patterns
- CDs: `AC9S1I04`
- Learn: sort/order data and represent patterns with provided tables and
  visual/physical models.
- Assignment: 6 questions (sort, table, simple model).

### Y1-SCI-I05 · Comparing predictions with observations
- CDs: `AC9S1I05`
- Learn: compare observations with predictions and others' observations;
  consider fairness; identify further questions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (compare, fairness, next question).

### Y1-SCI-I06 · Communicating observations
- CDs: `AC9S1I06`
- Learn: write/create texts to communicate observations, findings and ideas,
  using everyday and scientific vocabulary.
- Assignment: 6 questions (share findings, vocabulary).

## Year 5 — Science topics

### Y5-SCI-U01 · Adaptations for survival
- CDs: `AC9S5U01`
- Learn: structural features and behaviours of living things that enable
  survival in specific habitats.
- Assignment: 6 questions (structural/behavioural adaptations).

### Y5-SCI-U02 · Earth's changing surface
- CDs: `AC9S5U02`
- Learn: weathering, erosion, transportation and deposition cause slow or
  rapid change to Earth's surface.
- Assignment: 6 questions (weathering, erosion, deposition).

### Y5-SCI-U03 · Light
- CDs: `AC9S5U03`
- Learn: sources of light; light travels in straight paths; shadows; light
  reflects and refracts.
- Assignment: 6 questions (light sources, shadows, reflect/refract).

### Y5-SCI-U04 · Solids, liquids & gases (particles)
- CDs: `AC9S5U04`
- Learn: observable properties of solids, liquids and gases explained by
  modelling motion/arrangement of particles.
- Assignment: 6 questions (particle model, states of matter).

### Y5-SCI-H01 · Collaboration in science
- CDs: `AC9S5H01`
- Learn: advances in science often result from collaboration or build on the
  work of others.
- Assignment: 6 questions (collaboration, building on others).

### Y5-SCI-H02 · Science in decision-making
- CDs: `AC9S5H02`
- Learn: how scientific knowledge is used by individuals/communities to
  identify problems, consider responses, make decisions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (science-informed decisions).

### Y5-SCI-I01 · Investigable questions & predictions
- CDs: `AC9S5I01`
- Learn: pose investigable questions to identify patterns/test relationships;
  make reasoned predictions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (investigable questions, reasoned predictions).

### Y5-SCI-I02 · Planning repeatable investigations
- CDs: `AC9S5I02`
- Learn: plan/conduct repeatable investigations, deciding variables for fair
  tests, describing risks, safe material use.
- Assignment: 6 questions (repeatable, variables, risk).

### Y5-SCI-I03 · Precise measurement
- CDs: `AC9S5I03`
- Learn: use equipment to observe, measure and record data with reasonable
  precision, using digital tools.
- Assignment: 6 questions (precision, equipment, digital tools).

### Y5-SCI-I04 · Representing & describing patterns
- CDs: `AC9S5I04`
- Learn: construct/use representations (tables, graphs, models) to organise
  data and describe patterns/trends/relationships.
- Assignment: 6 questions (graphs, trends, relationships).

### Y5-SCI-I05 · Comparing methods & conclusions
- CDs: `AC9S5I05`
- Learn: compare methods/findings with others; recognise sources of error;
  pose further questions; draw reasoned conclusions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (error, conclude, compare).

### Y5-SCI-I06 · Communicating for audiences
- CDs: `AC9S5I06`
- Learn: write/create texts to communicate ideas/findings for specific
  purposes and audiences, selecting language features.
- Assignment: 6 questions (audience, purpose, language).
