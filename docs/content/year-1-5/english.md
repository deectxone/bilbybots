# Years 1–5 — English topics

Source of truth: Australian Curriculum v9.0, English F–10 (strands: Language LA,
Literature LE, Literacy LY). Every content description for Years 1–5 is covered
below and mirrored in `apps/mobile/src/data/year{1,2,3,4,5}/english.ts`.

Per AC v9.0, the Years 1–5 English band carries the following content
descriptions (codes verified against ACARA v9.0 F–6 curriculum content):

| Year | Language | Literature | Literacy | Total |
|------|---------:|-----------:|---------:|------:|
| 1    | 10       | 5          | 15       | 30    |
| 2    | 10       | 5          | 12       | 27    |
| 3    | 11       | 5          | 12       | 28    |
| 4    | 12       | 5          | 11       | 28    |
| 5    | 9        | 5          | 10       | 24    |

Each topic below is one unit (**lesson + quick-check + assignment**) tagged with
its CD codes. Topic ids follow `Y<year>-ENG-<strand><NN>`. Every topic ships 6
assignment questions at nominal pace and 4 under compact pace (adaptive pacing,
see `docs/specs/adaptive-pacing.md`). Illustration slots render real vector
diagrams via `src/components/illustrations/diagrams.tsx` (no emoji placeholders).

## Year 1 — English topics

### Y1-ENG-LA01 · Interaction: language, faces & gestures
- CDs: `AC9E1LA01`
- Learn: language, facial expressions and gestures used to interact when
  asking/providing information, offering, exclaiming, requesting, commanding.
- Assignment: 6 questions (identify purpose, match gesture/expression).

### Y1-ENG-LA02 · Language for likes, dislikes & preferences
- CDs: `AC9E1LA02`

- Assignment: 6 questions (express opinion, give reasons).

### Y1-ENG-LA03 · Text organisation by purpose
- CDs: `AC9E1LA03`
- Learn: how texts are organised according to purpose (recount, narrate,

- Assignment: 6 questions (match structure to purpose).

### Y1-ENG-LA04 · Repetition, rhyme & rhythm for cohesion
- CDs: `AC9E1LA04`
