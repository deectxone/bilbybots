# Year 6 — English topics

Source of truth: Australian Curriculum v9.0, English F–10 (strands: Language LA,
Literature LE, Literacy LY). All 23 Year‑6 content descriptions are covered below.

## Language (9 CDs)

### Y6-ENG-LA01 · Formal & informal language
- CDs: `AC9E6LA01`
- Learn: language varies as levels of formality and social distance increase;
  when we speak/write formally vs informally; texting vs email vs speech.
- Illustrations: two phone conversations side by side (mates vs principal),
  a register thermometer.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify/rewrite for formality).

### Y6-ENG-LA02 · Objective vs subjective language & bias
- CDs: `AC9E6LA02`
- Learn: uses of objective and subjective language; how to identify bias in
  writing; facts vs opinions.
- Illustrations: a robot (objective) and an excited fan (subjective) reporting
  the same game.
- Assignment: 8 questions (sort factual/opinion, spot bias words).

### Y6-ENG-LA03 · Text structure & stages
- CDs: `AC9E6LA03`
- Learn: how texts across the curriculum are organised into characteristic
  stages and phases for their purpose; authors adapt text structures and
  language features.
- Illustrations: labelled "skeleton" of a report, a narrative mountain, a
  persuasion scaffold.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify features/purpose from structure).

### Y6-ENG-LA04 · Cohesion & word associations
- CDs: `AC9E6LA04`
- Learn: cohesion via intentional repetition and word associations.
- Illustrations: linking-chain diagrams, highlighted signal words in a passage.
- Assignment: 8 questions (improve cohesion, find linking devices).

### Y6-ENG-LA05 · Embedded clauses & complex sentences
- CDs: `AC9E6LA05`
- Learn: how embedded clauses expand the variety of complex sentences to
  elaborate, extend and explain ideas.
- Illustrations: train-car sentence diagrams, clauses packed as suitcases.
- Assignment: 8 questions (build/expand complex sentences).

### Y6-ENG-LA06 · Verb choice, tenses & adverb groups
- CDs: `AC9E6LA06`
- Learn: ideas sharpened through careful choice of verbs, elaborated tenses and
  a range of adverb groups.
- Illustrations: paint-palette of strong verbs, glow adverbs in a scene.
- Assignment: 8 questions (upgrade verb/adverb choices, tense consistency).

### Y6-ENG-LA07 · Images, figures, tables, diagrams, maps & graphs
- CDs: `AC9E6LA07`
- Learn: how illustrations and data displays contribute to meaning in texts.
- Illustrations: annotated textbook page, weather map, bar graph, diagram.
- Assignment: 8 questions (explain what a visual adds).

### Y6-ENG-LA08 · Vivid & emotive vocabulary (figurative language)
- CDs: `AC9E6LA08`
- Learn: authors' use of vivid, emotive vocabulary — metaphors, similes,
  personification, idioms, imagery and hyperbole.
- Illustrations: an "exaggeration island" for hyperbole, a metaphor cloud,
  idioms literally vs figuratively.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify & explain each device).

### Y6-ENG-LA09 · The comma
- CDs: `AC9E6LA09`
- Learn: comma for lists, separating a dependent clause from an independent
  clause, and in dialogue.
- Illustrations: stop-and-go signposts in sentences; dialogue balloons.
- Assignment: 8 questions (insert/correct commas).

## Literature (5 CDs)

### Y6-ENG-LE01 · Responding to literature across contexts
- CDs: `AC9E6LE01`
- Learn: responses to characters and events in literary texts drawn from
  historical, social or cultural contexts — by First Nations Australians and
  wide-ranging Australian and world authors.
- Illustrations: book covers from diverse Australian/First Nations/world
  authors; a "context postcard".
- Assignment: 8 questions (connect context to story response).

### Y6-ENG-LE02 · Similarities & differences across texts
- CDs: `AC9E6LE02`
- Learn: identifying similarities and differences in literary texts on similar
  topics, themes or plots.
- Illustrations: Venn diagrams of two fairy tales, film-vs-book scenes.
- Assignment: 8 questions (compare two texts on topic/theme/plot).

### Y6-ENG-LE03 · An author's style
- CDs: `AC9E6LE03`
- Learn: identifying and explaining characteristics that define an author's
  individual style.
- Illustrations: "author fingerprints" — sentence rhythm, word choice, humour.
- Assignment: 8 questions (attribute style traits to passages).

### Y6-ENG-LE04 · Sound & imagery in poetry
- CDs: `AC9E6LE04`
- Learn: the way authors use sound (rhyme, rhythm, alliteration, onomatopoeia)
  and imagery to create meaning and effect in poetry.
- Illustrations: stormy sea poem with sound-words; imagery hotspots.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify sound devices, explain imagery effect).

### Y6-ENG-LE05 · Creating literary texts
- CDs: `AC9E6LE05`
- Learn: create and edit literary texts that adapt plot structure, characters,
  settings and/or ideas from texts experienced; experiment with literary devices.
- Illustrations: a storyboard planner; a "borrow & twist" idea lab.
- Assignment: 8 questions (plan, adapt, apply devices, self-edit checklist).

## Literacy (9 CDs)

### Y6-ENG-LY01 · Texts & their contexts
- CDs: `AC9E6LY01`
- Learn: examine texts, including media texts, that represent ideas and events;
  identify how they reflect the context in which they were created.
- Illustrations: a newspaper front page then-and-now, an old ad vs a new ad.
- Assignment: 8 questions (link text features to time/place/audience).

### Y6-ENG-LY02 · Interaction & discussion skills
- CDs: `AC9E6LY02`
- Learn: use interaction skills and awareness of formality when paraphrasing,
  questioning, clarifying and interrogating ideas; developing and supporting
  arguments; sharing/evaluating information, experiences and opinions.
- Illustrations: conversation turn-taking cards, a classroom fishbowl debate.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify good moves, rewrite responses).

### Y6-ENG-LY03 · Analysing text structures & features for purpose
- CDs: `AC9E6LY03`
- Learn: analyse how text structures and language features work together to
  meet a text's purpose and engage and influence audiences.
- Illustrations: colour-coded persuasive advert, labels on an info-text.
- Assignment: 8 questions (map structure → purpose → audience).

### Y6-ENG-LY04 · Reading with strategy — selecting & navigating texts
- CDs: `AC9E6LY04`
- Learn: select, navigate and read texts for a range of purposes, monitoring
  meaning and evaluating structural features (table of contents, glossary,
  chapters, headings, subheadings).
- Illustrations: a textbook explorer, a contents/glossary treasure map.
- Assignment: 8 questions (use structural features to find information).

### Y6-ENG-LY05 · Comprehension strategies
- CDs: `AC9E6LY05`
- Learn: use comprehension strategies — visualising, predicting, connecting,
  summarising, monitoring and questioning — to build literal and inferred
  meaning across a variety of sources.
- Illustrations: six strategy icons applied to a short passage.
- Assignment: 8 questions (infer, summarise, predict, monitor).

### Y6-ENG-LY06 · Writing & publishing multimodal texts
- CDs: `AC9E6LY06`
- Learn: plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts
  (imaginative, informative, persuasive) using paragraphs, complex sentences,
  expanded verb groups, tense, topic-specific and vivid vocabulary, punctuation
  and spelling, and visual features.
- Illustrations: writing-process wheel, paragraph builder, editing checklist.
- Assignment: 8 questions (sequence text, revise drafts, add features).

### Y6-ENG-LY07 · Spoken & multimodal presentations
- CDs: `AC9E6LY07`
- Learn: plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations
  with information, arguments and details; organising ideas with precise
  topic-specific vocabulary, pitch, tone, pace, volume, and visual/digital features.
- Illustrations: presentation pyramid, rehearsal mirror, slide thumbnails.
- Assignment: 8 questions (plan a talk, improve delivery choices).

### Y6-ENG-LY08 · Phonic knowledge for complex words
- CDs: `AC9E6LY08`
- Learn: use phonic knowledge of common and less common grapheme–phoneme
  relationships to read and write increasingly complex words.
- Illustrations: grapheme cards, tricky-word vault, word-building bricks.
- Assignment: 8 questions (decode/encode complex words).

### Y6-ENG-LY09 · Spelling — word origins & word-building
- CDs: `AC9E6LY09`
- Learn: use knowledge of known words, word origins (incl. Latin/Greek roots),
  base words, prefixes, suffixes, letter patterns and spelling generalisations
  to spell new words, incl. technical words.
- Illustrations: roots chart (aqua=water, photo=light), prefix/suffix machines.
- Assignment: 8 questions (apply roots/affixes, correct spellings).
