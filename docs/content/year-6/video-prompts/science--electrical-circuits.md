# Video prompt — Y6-SCI-U03 · Electrical energy & circuits

Curriculum: investigate the transfer and transformation of energy in electrical
circuits, including the role of circuit components, insulators and conductors
(`AC9S6U03`).

## Scene 1 — Hook (≤8 s)
### Visual
> Cozy bedroom dusk, Phase-1 illustration style. Bilby switches on a bedside
> lamp; warm light floods the room. Animated arcing energy doodle travels from
> a power point to the lamp.
### Voiceover
> "Flip the switch, and blink — light! But how does electricity make it
> happen? Let's build a circuit and find out."

## Scene 2 — Explain (≤12 s)
### Visual
> Simple circuit diagram animates: battery → wires → bulb → back to battery, in
> a closed loop. Energy flow shown as glowing dots moving around the loop; the
> bulb lights when dots pass. A conductor (metal key) lets dots flow; an
> insulator (rubber glove) blocks them.
### Voiceover
> "Electricity needs a full loop called a circuit. Conductors let the flow
> through. Insulators stop it — that's why wiring wears rubber!"

## Scene 3 — Example (≤8 s)
### Visual
> Split experiment: left a paper-clip joins the gap, bulb glows; right a wooden
> ruler joins the gap, bulb stays dark. "Conductor" / "Insulator" tags pop.
### Voiceover
> "Metal nail — light! Wooden ruler — nothing! Test it, and you'll know which
> is which."

## Scene 4 — Recap (≤8 s)
### Visual
> Bilby grins by a glowing bulb; three cards: CIRCUIT (closed loop), CONDUCTOR
> (lets it flow), INSULATOR (stops it). Energy transform symbol: ⚡→💡 note.
### Voiceover
> "Circuits make a loop, conductors carry the flow, insulators keep us safe.
> Science power — switched ON!"
