# Year 6 — Science topics

Source of truth: Australian Curriculum v9.0, Science F–10 (strands: Science
Understanding SU, Science as a Human Endeavour H, Science Inquiry I). All 12
Year‑6 content descriptions are covered below.

## Science understanding (4 CDs)

### Y6-SCI-U01 · Habitats & living things
- CDs: `AC9S6U01`
- Learn: investigate the physical conditions of a habitat and analyse how the
  growth and survival of living things is affected by changing conditions
  (temperature, water, light, food, shelter).
- Illustrations: a desert/rainforest/reef habitat panel; a "conditions meter"
  for a plant in a sun/water experiment.
- Assignment: 8 questions (predict/explain how conditions affect survival).

### Y6-SCI-U02 · Earth, Sun & planets — day, night & seasons
- CDs: `AC9S6U02`
- Learn: describe the movement of Earth and other planets relative to the Sun;
  model how Earth's tilt, rotation on its axis and revolution around the Sun
  relate to cyclic phenomena, incl. variable day and night length.
- Illustrations: orrery/solar-system model, a globe-torch daylight demo,
  seasons diagram from Earth's tilt.
- Assignment: 8 questions (explain day/night, seasons, day-length differences).

### Y6-SCI-U03 · Electrical energy & circuits
- CDs: `AC9S6U03`
- Learn: investigate transfer and transformation of energy in electrical
  circuits, incl. the role of circuit components, insulators and conductors.
- Illustrations: battery-bulb circuit; insulators vs conductors labelled; an
  energy-flow cartoon (chemical → electrical → light/heat/sound).
- Assignment: 8 questions (build/read circuits, classify conductor/insulator).

### Y6-SCI-U04 · Reversible & irreversible changes
- CDs: `AC9S6U04`
- Learn: compare reversible changes (incl. dissolving, changes of state) and
  irreversible changes (incl. cooking, rusting) that produce new substances.
- Illustrations: ice/water/steam cycle; cooking a pancake; rusty nail timeline;
  sugar dissolving in water.
- Assignment: 8 questions (classify changes, explain why new substances).

## Science as a human endeavour (2 CDs)

### Y6-SCI-H01 · Science is collaborative
- CDs: `AC9S6H01`
- Learn: examine why advances in science are often the result of collaboration
  or build on the work of others (past & present, various cultures).
- Illustrations: a scientists' timeline showing teamwork (e.g. dinosaur digs,
  vaccine teams, First Nations astronomy & knowledge).
- Assignment: 8 questions (match contributions, explain collaboration).

### Y6-SCI-H02 · Science in everyday decisions
- CDs: `AC9S6H02`
- Learn: investigate how scientific knowledge is used by individuals and
  communities to identify problems, consider responses and make decisions.
- Illustrations: coastal erosion response, recycling policy, choosing sunscreen,
  drought-resistant farming.
- Assignment: 8 questions (link knowledge → problem → decision).

## Science inquiry (6 CDs)

### Y6-SCI-I01 · Asking questions & predicting
- CDs: `AC9S6I01`
- Learn: pose investigable questions to identify patterns and test
  relationships; make reasoned predictions.
- Illustrations: question-ladder (from "what" to "how/if"), a prediction star.
- Assignment: 6 questions (turn ideas into testable questions, predict).

### Y6-SCI-I02 · Planning fair tests
- CDs: `AC9S6I02`
- Learn: plan and conduct repeatable investigations — deciding variables to
  change, measure and control in fair tests; describing risks; planning safe
  equipment use; identifying permissions for investigations on Country/Place.
- Illustrations: a "fair test" lab bench showing changed/measured/controlled
  jars for a plant experiment; a safety-vest checklist.
- Assignment: 6 questions (identify variables, plan safe steps).

### Y6-SCI-I03 · Measuring & recording data
- CDs: `AC9S6I03`
- Learn: use equipment to observe, measure and record data with reasonable
  precision, using digital tools as appropriate.
- Illustrations: ruler/thermometer/timer close-ups; a data table and a
  measurement log.
- Assignment: 6 questions (choose units, record precisely).

### Y6-SCI-I04 · Representing & describing data
- CDs: `AC9S6I04`
- Learn: construct and use appropriate representations (tables, graphs, visual
  or physical models) to organise and process data and describe patterns,
  trends and relationships.
- Illustrations: steps from tally → column graph → "what it means" caption.
- Assignment: 6 questions (build/read displays, describe patterns).

### Y6-SCI-I05 · Evaluating — errors & evidence
- CDs: `AC9S6I05`
- Learn: compare methods and findings with others; recognise possible sources of
  error; pose further questions; select evidence for reasoned conclusions.
- Illustrations: a "spot the wobble" measuring-mistake panel; evidence cards vs
  opinions.
- Assignment: 6 questions (spot error sources, choose supporting evidence).

### Y6-SCI-I06 · Communicating findings
- CDs: `AC9S6I06`
- Learn: write and create texts to communicate ideas and findings for specific
  purposes and audiences, incl. selection of language features, using digital
  tools as appropriate.
- Illustrations: a mini scientific-poster template; a "say it well" word bank.
- Assignment: 6 questions (summarise findings for an audience).
