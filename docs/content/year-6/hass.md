# Year 6 — HASS topics (History, Geography, Civics & Citizenship, Economics & Business)

Source of truth: Australian Curriculum v9.0, HASS F–6 (strands: Knowledge &
understanding K, Skills S). Focus for Year 6: **"Australia in the past and
present, and its connections with a diverse world."** All 8 knowledge CDs and
the 7 skills CDs (skills taught integrated with knowledge units) are covered.

> Skills CDs (`AC9HS6S01–S07`) are taught **within** the knowledge topics below
> (an inquiry is embedded in each unit), matching how schools program HASS.

## History (3 CDs)

### Y6-HASS-HI01 · Federation & the Constitution
- CDs: `AC9HS6K01`
- Learn: significant individuals, events and ideas that led to Australia's
  Federation, the Constitution and the democratic system of government.
- Illustrations: 1901 celebration, an "old map" of colonies, the six states
  agreeing, key figures portrait gallery.
- Assignment: 8 questions + an inquiry task (research a key figure).
- Skills woven in: `AC9HS6S01` questioning, `AC9HS6S02` locate/organise sources,
  `AC9HS6S03` infer relationships.

### Y6-HASS-HI02 · Citizenship & democracy though the 20th century
- CDs: `AC9HS6K02`
- Learn: changes in Australia's political system and citizenship after
  Federation and throughout the 20th century that affected First Nations
  Australians, migrants, women and children.
- Illustrations: a citizenship timeline (voting rights, citizenship for First
  Nations Australians, women's voting), a "then…now" contrast page.
- Assignment: 8 questions (sequence changes, explain impact on groups).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S03`/`AC9HS6S04` evaluate sources (origin, purpose, perspectives).
- ⚠ `sensitivity: review` — First Nations Australians' experiences; consult
  ACARA/community protocols.

### Y6-HASS-HI03 · Migration to Australia since Federation
- CDs: `AC9HS6K03`
- Learn: the motivation of people migrating to Australia since Federation and
  throughout the 20th century, their stories and effects on Australian society,
  incl. migrants from the Asia region.
- Illustrations: migration journey map, suitcase of personal belongings, a
  "stories wall" of families, Asia-region spotlight.
- Assignment: 8 questions (causes/effects, a family-migration case study).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S04` evaluate perspectives, `AC9HS6S05` evidence-based conclusions.

## Geography (2 CDs)

### Y6-HASS-GE01 · The Asia region
- CDs: `AC9HS6K04`
- Learn: geographical diversity and location of places in the Asia region, and
  its location in relation to Australia.
- Illustrations: a big Asia map with Australia beside it; photographs of
  varied landscapes/cities; a compass/scale lesson.
- Assignment: 8 questions (locate/describe diversity, relative location).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S02`, `AC9HS6S07` present with relevant terms & conventions.

### Y6-HASS-GE02 · Australia's interconnections with other countries
- CDs: `AC9HS6K05`
- Learn: Australia's interconnections with other countries and how these change
  people and places (trade, travel, tourism, environment, family links).
- Illustrations: a "connections web" (exports, holidays, food, sport), a world
  map threaded with arrows.
- Assignment: 8 questions (trace an interconnection, explain its effects).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S03`/`AC9HS6S06` propose responses, assess effects.

## Civics & Citizenship (2 CDs)

### Y6-HASS-CV01 · Democratic institutions & Westminster system
- CDs: `AC9HS6K06`
- Learn: key institutions of Australia's system of government, how it is based
  on the Westminster system, and the key values/beliefs of Western democracies
  (Parliament, Executive, Judiciary; democratic values).
- Illustrations: Parliament House cutaway (Parliament/Executive/Judiciary),
  Westminster origins diagram, a "values compass" (fairness, equality, freedom).
- Assignment: 8 questions (match institutions to roles, identify values).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S01`, `AC9HS6S05`.

### Y6-HASS-CV02 · Three levels of government
- CDs: `AC9HS6K07`
- Learn: roles and responsibilities of the 3 levels of government in Australia
  (Federal, State/Territory, Local).
- Illustrations: a three-layer cake diagram labelled with services each layer
  runs (defence, schools, parks).
- Assignment: 8 questions (sort services to levels, explain responsibilities).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S07` communicate using relevant terms.

## Economics & Business (1 CD)

### Y6-HASS-EC01 · Consumer & financial choices
- CDs: `AC9HS6K08`
- Learn: influences on consumer choices and strategies to help make informed
  personal consumer and financial choices.
- Illustrations: a shop aisle with influence arrows (ads, price, friends,
  quality), a save/spend/give decision wheel, a simple budget sheet.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify influences, apply choice strategies).
- Skills: `AC9HS6S01`, `AC9HS6S06` propose actions & assess effects.

## Skills strand — cross-cutting
The seven skills CDs are distributed across the units above as "inquiry
moments":
- `AC9HS6S01` Questioning and researching — pose questions to investigate.
- `AC9HS6S02` Locate, collect and organise information/data from sources.
- `AC9HS6S03` Interpret/analyse to identify patterns, trends, relationships.
- `AC9HS6S04` Evaluate sources (origin, purpose, perspectives).
- `AC9HS6S05` Develop evidence-based conclusions.
- `AC9HS6S06` Propose actions/responses and assess possible effects.
- `AC9HS6S07` Communicate descriptions/explanations with relevant terms.
