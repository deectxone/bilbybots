# Year 6 — Mathematics topics

Source of truth: Australian Curriculum v9.0, Mathematics F–6 (strands: Number,
Algebra, Measurement, Space, Statistics, Probability). All 24 Year‑6 content
descriptions are covered below.

> Topic ids: `Y6-MAT-NN##`, `Y6-MAT-AL##`, `Y6-MAT-ME##`, `Y6-MAT-SP##`,
> `Y6-MAT-ST##`, `Y6-MAT-PR##`.

## Number (9 CDs)

### Y6-MAT-NN01 · Whole numbers up to millions & place value
- CDs: `AC9M6N01`
- Learn: reading, writing, comparing and ordering whole numbers to millions;
  place value chart; integers and negative numbers (below zero, temperature,
  banking/finance), number line, Cartesian plane coordinates (introduction to
  quadrants I–IV).
- Illustrations: a big place-value house; a thermometer below zero; a football
  teams' points table; a grid showing the 4 quadrants.
- Assignment: 8 questions (order numbers, locate integers, read coordinates).

### Y6-MAT-NN02 · Prime, composite & square numbers
- CDs: `AC9M6N02`
- Learn: what makes a number prime/composite/square; factor pairs; using
  properties to simplify calculations (e.g. breaking numbers into factors).
- Illustrations: brick walls built from factors; "sieve" grid; square number
  dot arrays.
- Assignment: 8 questions (classify, list factors, complete factor statements).

### Y6-MAT-NN03 · Fractions — equivalence, comparing & ordering
- CDs: `AC9M6N03`
- Learn: equivalent fractions; comparing and ordering halves, thirds, quarters
  and other common fractions; common denominators; same-number-line ordering.
- Illustrations: fraction bars and pie slices; two friends sharing candy bars.
- Assignment: 8 questions (equivalence, ordering, missing numerators).

### Y6-MAT-NN04 · Adding & subtracting decimals
- CDs: `AC9M6N04`
- Learn: place value in decimals; column addition/subtraction with money;
  estimation and rounding to check reasonableness; using digital tools.
- Illustrations: cash registers, a price board, a running track measuring.
- Assignment: 8 questions (add/sub decimals to 2dp, estimate to check).

### Y6-MAT-NN05 · Adding & subtracting fractions
- CDs: `AC9M6N05`
- Learn: addition/subtraction of fractions using equivalent fractions
  (like and related denominators); solve word problems.
- Illustrations: pizza and cake-sharing problems done step-by-step.
- Assignment: 8 questions (compute sums/differences, word problems).

### Y6-MAT-NN06 · Multiplying & dividing decimals by powers of ten
- CDs: `AC9M6N06`
- Learn: mental multiplication/division by 10, 100, 1000 (digits shift,
  not "add zeros"); estimation to check answers; shopping and travel contexts.
- Illustrations: digit cards sliding left/right on a place-value mat; a
  checkout receipt.
- Assignment: 8 questions (×/÷ by 10/100/1000, word problems, estimation).

### Y6-MAT-NN07 · Percentages, discounts & fractions of a quantity
- CDs: `AC9M6N07`
- Learn: a fraction, decimal or percentage of a quantity (e.g. ⅓ of 90, 25% of
  80); percentage discounts in shops; efficient strategies; digital tools.
- Illustrations: a sale sign, splitting a team into groups, number lines for
  %, fractions, decimals.
- Assignment: 8 questions (find % of amount, discounts, reverse-style checks).

### Y6-MAT-NN08 · Estimation & approximation
- CDs: `AC9M6N08`
- Learn: approximating solutions to problems involving rational numbers and
  percentages; financial contexts; when approximate is good enough.
- Illustrations: rounding benchmark chart; budgeting a trip; estimating a bill.
- Assignment: 8 questions (estimate answers, explain "about how many").

### Y6-MAT-NN09 · Mathematical modelling (real problems)
- CDs: `AC9M6N09`
- Learn: using modelling to solve practical problems with natural and rational
  numbers and percentages, incl. financial contexts; formulating problems,
  choosing operations/strategies, interpreting and communicating the solution.
- Illustrations: a mini-project (plan a school fundraiser) following the
  modelling process boxes.
- Assignment: 8 questions (given scenario → model → solve → explain).

## Algebra (3 CDs)

### Y6-MAT-AL01 · Patterns & rules
- CDs: `AC9M6A01`
- Learn: rules that generate visually growing patterns and number patterns
  involving rational numbers (e.g. 1/2, 1, 1 1/2, 2 …).
- Illustrations: growing staircases and bead patterns; tables mapping term → value.
- Assignment: 8 questions (find rule, predict next terms).

### Y6-MAT-AL02 · Unknown values & order of operations
- CDs: `AC9M6A02`
- Learn: find unknown values in numerical equations involving brackets and
  combinations of the four operations; properties of numbers/operations;
  correct order of operations.
- Illustrations: balance scales with mystery numbers; "brackets first" traffic
  lights.
- Assignment: 8 questions (solve equations, order-of-operations puzzles).

### Y6-MAT-AL03 · Algorithms (sequences & decisions)
- CDs: `AC9M6A03`
- Learn: create and use algorithms — sequences of steps and decisions — to
  generate number sequences and solve problems.
- Illustrations: flowcharts with diamonds (decisions) and boxes (steps).
- Assignment: 8 questions (follow/trace an algorithm, build one).

## Measurement (4 CDs)

### Y6-MAT-ME01 · Converting metric units
- CDs: `AC9M6M01`
- Learn: convert between common metric units of length, mass and capacity;
  choose decimal representations of metric measurements for a problem.
- Illustrations: a metric ladder (km→m→cm→mm, kg→g, L→mL), kitchen and sport scales.
- Assignment: 8 questions (conversions, choose sensible unit, decimals).

### Y6-MAT-ME02 · Area of a rectangle
- CDs: `AC9M6M02`
- Learn: establish the formula `area = length × width`; apply to solve
  practical problems (gardens, rooms, tiles).
- Illustrations: tiled floors, a park rectangle, a baking tray.
- Assignment: 8 questions (find area, missing side, real-world problems).

### Y6-MAT-ME03 · Timetables & itineraries
- CDs: `AC9M6M03`
- Learn: interpret and use timetables and itineraries to plan activities and
  determine duration of events and journeys.
- Illustrations: a train/bus timetable, a day itinerary, elapsed-time number lines.
- Assignment: 8 questions (read timetable, compute durations, plan a trip).

### Y6-MAT-ME04 · Angles — relationships
- CDs: `AC9M6M04`
- Learn: angles on a straight line sum to 180°, angles at a point to 360°,
  vertically opposite angles are equal; find unknown angles with reasoning.
- Illustrations: a clock, a turning roundabout, intersecting streets showing
  opposite angles.
- Assignment: 8 questions (find missing angles, explain reasoning).

## Space (3 CDs)

### Y6-MAT-SP01 · Cross-sections & right prisms
- CDs: `AC9M6SP01`
- Learn: compare parallel cross-sections of objects and recognise their
  relationship to right prisms.
- Illustrations: slicing a chocolate block, clay shapes, cross-section shapes.
- Assignment: 8 questions (predict/compare cross-sections).

### Y6-MAT-SP02 · Cartesian plane — four quadrants
- CDs: `AC9M6SP02`
- Learn: locate points in all 4 quadrants; describe how coordinates change when
  a point moves in the plane.
- Illustrations: map grid, treasure hunt, robot graphing arms.
- Assignment: 8 questions (read/plot coordinates, describe movement).

### Y6-MAT-SP03 · Transformations & tessellations
- CDs: `AC9M6SP03`
- Learn: combine transformations (translation, reflection, rotation) to create
  tessellations and other geometric patterns; digital geometry tools.
- Illustrations: wallpaper patterns, floor tiles inspired by Islamic/Māori
  patterns, rotating/reflecting stamps.
- Assignment: 8 questions (identify transformation combos, complete tessellation).

## Statistics (3 CDs)

### Y6-MAT-ST01 · Comparing data sets
- CDs: `AC9M6ST01`
- Learn: interpret and compare data sets (ordinal and nominal categorical,
  discrete and continuous numerical) using comparative displays/visualisations;
  compare distributions in terms of mode, range and shape.
- Illustrations: side-by-side column graphs, dot plots, back-to-back stem plots.
- Assignment: 8 questions (read displays, compare mode/range/shape).

### Y6-MAT-ST02 · Statistics in the media
- CDs: `AC9M6ST02`
- Learn: identify statistically informed arguments in traditional and digital
  media; discuss and critique methods, data representations and conclusions.
- Illustrations: commercial "9 out of 10 dentists" style charts, news graphs.
- Assignment: 8 questions (spot misleading graph, critique claim).

### Y6-MAT-ST03 · Statistical investigations
- CDs: `AC9M6ST03`
- Learn: plan and conduct a statistical investigation — pose/refine questions,
  collect data, analyse/interpret, communicate findings.
- Illustrations: survey → tally → graph → conclusion flow.
- Assignment: 8 questions (design step, interpret own/others' data).

## Probability (2 CDs)

### Y6-MAT-PR01 · Probability scales
- CDs: `AC9M6P01`
- Learn: probabilities lie on scales 0–1 or 0%–100%; use estimation to assign
  probabilities to events (impossible…certain).
- Illustrations: a probability meter, spinners, weather forecast percentages.
- Assignment: 8 questions (place events on scale, estimate probabilities).

### Y6-MAT-PR02 · Chance experiments & simulations
- CDs: `AC9M6P02`
- Learn: conduct repeated chance experiments and run simulations with an
  increasing number of trials; compare observed vs predicted frequencies.
- Illustrations: coin/dice sweeps, a simple digital spinner simulation.
- Assignment: 8 questions (predict, run record, compare frequencies).
